{{#_}}PB funding of $500,000 can be used to support projects that benefit the public in Cambridge. Successful ideas will answer the following questions:

  1. What need does the idea address?
  2. What kind of positive impact would it have?
  3. Is it feasible for the City to do? (i.e. Does it follow City laws? What would it take to do this idea? What would the money do for the idea?)
For more information about the projects that are eligible for PB, please review the guidelines.{{/_}}
